# 95-second cut

For when the clock is the constraint. Two tabs only: the home page and a terminal in `app/`.
One take, no editing. If you fluff a line, keep going — judges forgive a stumble, not a missing video.

---

### 0:00 – 0:12 · Hook
*Home page. Let the live prices land.*

> Wall Street shuts at four o'clock. Base does not.
>
> This is Gameweek — a fantasy league where the players are real tokenized stocks, and the
> transfer market never closes.

### 0:12 – 0:30 · The number that only exists here
*Point at a card's percentage chip.*

> Every card shows a live price from an onchain pool, and how far it has drifted from Friday's
> close. That gap is the whole idea. It only exists because these stocks keep trading after the
> exchange goes home for the weekend.

### 0:30 – 0:55 · The game and the chain
*Scroll the board. Don't draft — describing it is faster than doing it.*

> You field five, in a one-two-two. One pick wears the armband, and the captain's stake is
> doubled onchain — a real position size, not a multiplier bolted onto a scoreboard.
>
> A pick is a real swap into your own wallet, and a whole draft is one signature. The router
> takes fifty basis points, and that fee funds the pot.
>
> Twenty-four hours later the contract reads Chainlink, ranks the league, and pays the top
> three sixty, thirty, ten. Both contracts are live and verified on Base mainnet.

### 0:55 – 1:25 · The proof
*Terminal. Run `node scripts/prove-live.mjs`. Let it scroll. Hold on the summary.*

> Now the honest part. No round has been played — the wallet holding the float became
> unreachable before the first one could open, and a round with nothing staked cannot settle,
> by design.
>
> So instead of showing you a result I did not get, everything a round depends on is proven
> against live mainnet. This sends no transaction and spends nothing.
>
> It buys a quarter of a dollar of Nvidia through the real pool, and lands within a sixth of a
> percent of the Chainlink price. Then it opens, locks and settles a whole round against real
> feeds with the clock moved forward. Sixteen checks, all green. Sixty-four unit tests pass.

### 1:25 – 1:35 · Close
*Back to the home page.*

> Opening a real round is one funded transaction away, and it costs two tenths of a cent.
>
> Gameweek. A stock draft on a Sunday — the one thing a brokerage cannot give you.

## If you have literally ten minutes

Cut to 0:00–0:30 and 0:55–1:25. Hook plus proof. That is still a valid, honest submission.

## Do not

- Connect a wallet on camera. The popup needs Coinbase hosts and a network block ends up on tape.
- Re-record for a stumble. Ship the take.
- Skip the honest section to save time. It is the most persuasive thirty seconds in the video.
